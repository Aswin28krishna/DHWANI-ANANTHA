About Dhwani–Anantha

Everyone carries a note within them worth hearing — and a community that sings together, heals together. Dhwani–Anantha is a Thiruvananthapuram collective that finds hidden voices and gives them the stage they deserve.

Classical to contemporary, veena to guitar, solo to ensemble — every language, every genre, every singer finds a home here. Monthly meets, annual concerts, workshops, and a warm community await you.

Music heals the soul — give it a stage, and it heals a hundred more.
