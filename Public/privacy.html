Privacy notice

What we collect

When you apply for membership we collect the details you enter: name, age and occupation, address, phone or WhatsApp number, email, the category you want, and who introduced you.

Why we collect it

To process your application, contact you about membership, fees and events, and add you to the member WhatsApp group.

Who sees it

Only the Dhwani–Anantha organisers handling applications. We do not sell your details. Our form and payment providers process data on our behalf.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your details at any time by writing to the contact address in the site footer.

Organisers: please review this notice with your own advisor before launch.
